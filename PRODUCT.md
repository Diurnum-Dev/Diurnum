# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Diurnum serves technically literate solo business owners who run their own books. The primary Founder-Operator is comfortable with text files, terminal tools, and version control, and wants trustworthy financial statements without opaque accounting workflows. They use the app in focused bookkeeping sessions: importing statements, reviewing proposed entries, maintaining a readable Beancount ledger, and checking reports.

## Product Purpose

Diurnum is a local-first, open-source desktop accounting workspace built around plain Beancount-compatible files the user owns. It helps Founder-Operators turn imported financial activity into a clean, valid, traceable ledger. AI and automation may propose changes, but people explicitly review and approve every ledger write. Success means the user can understand what changed, trust the resulting books, reverse mistakes, and leave with portable files.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, and confident. Diurnum should feel like a composed macOS productivity tool for serious work: dense but breathable, quietly authoritative, and fast enough to stay out of the user's way.

## Anti-references

Avoid QuickBooks-like opacity and sprawling click paths, AI-first branding that makes automation the product, generic SaaS dashboard decoration, gradients, heavy shadows, gratuitous motion, and unfamiliar controls invented for visual novelty. The interface should never feel like a financial cockpit or obscure the readable ledger underneath it.

## Design Principles

1. The ledger is the product; every interface should help maintain a trustworthy, readable ledger.
2. AI proposes and humans approve; make review, provenance, and reversibility unmistakable.
3. Show the source and the consequence; financial actions should be traceable before and after approval.
4. Optimize for keyboard-driven flow without compromising familiar, accessible controls.
5. Prefer calm clarity over decoration; information can be dense, but hierarchy and whitespace must keep it legible.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Support complete keyboard operation, visible focus, meaningful accessible names and landmarks, sufficient contrast without relying on color alone, screen-reader-compatible status updates, and reduced-motion preferences. Dense accounting views must remain understandable at narrow window sizes and under text zoom.
