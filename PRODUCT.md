# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Diurnum is for the Founder-Operator: a technically literate solo business owner who runs their own books and wants accounting records they can inspect, version, and understand. They work in a local macOS desktop app, are comfortable with text files and git, and use the Inbox to turn imported bank rows into trustworthy ledger entries with as little repetitive review as possible.

## Product Purpose

Diurnum is a local-first, FOSS accounting workspace built around a portable Beancount ledger. It helps a Founder-Operator import raw bank data, review transparent suggestions, approve valid ledger changes, and keep a durable record they own. Success means the common accounting loop is fast without becoming opaque: automation may prepare a change, but only the human can place it in the ledger.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, confident. The product should feel like a well-kept scholar's desk: literate and composed, with the speed and familiarity of a native macOS tool. Its voice is plainspoken and exact, never breezy fintech copy, classical pastiche, or AI-first hype.

## Anti-references

Diurnum should not resemble a consumer budgeting dashboard, a gamified expense tracker, a colorful fintech cockpit, or an autonomous AI bookkeeping agent. Avoid opaque category-only actions, hidden file mutation, cloud-account pressure, decorative automation, modal-heavy flows, and interfaces that obscure the underlying ledger or provenance.

## Design Principles

1. The ledger is the product. Every surface should improve the quality, clarity, or maintenance of the Beancount record.
2. AI proposes; humans approve. Suggestions are visible, bounded, editable, and never silently committed.
3. Make trust inspectable. Show source text, proposed changes, validation state, provenance, and reversal paths where decisions are made.
4. Keep skilled users in flow. Prefer dense, keyboard-friendly, progressive interfaces over wizards and repeated confirmation.
5. Preserve local ownership. The product must remain useful without a hosted account, and accounting data stays in files the Founder-Operator controls.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Core workflows must remain operable by keyboard and expose semantic labels, visible focus states, predictable reading order, and non-color state cues. Text and controls should meet WCAG AA contrast targets. Motion must communicate state, stay brief, and honor reduced-motion preferences. Dense accounting data must remain legible under text zoom and narrow-window layouts without hiding approval or recovery actions.
